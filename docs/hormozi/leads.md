# $100M Leads: working notes

Our own condensed notes on the ideas in Alex Hormozi's book, written to guide Eka's reviews. They paraphrase the concepts and are not a substitute for reading the book. The compressed version used by the review prompt lives in `lib/review/lens.ts`.

## The core idea

Nobody gets customers without leads, and leads come from a small number of channels done consistently. The skill is making people aware of you, giving value before asking, and following up. Most businesses do not have a lead problem so much as a "nobody knows us, nobody asked us, and we never followed up" problem.

## Lead magnets

A lead magnet is a free, useful thing offered in exchange for contact details. Good ones:

- Solve one narrow, real problem fast, and reveal the next problem that the paid service solves.
- Are specific (a one-page checklist, a quote calculator, a short audit, a template), not "sign up for our newsletter".
- Ask for the minimum details. A phone number or WhatsApp number often beats an email in this market.
- Deliver value quickly, ideally the same day.

## The four ways to get leads (the "Core Four")

1. **Warm outreach:** contact people who already know you (past customers, friends, contacts). Fastest and cheapest to start.
2. **Posting free content:** teach, show results, answer questions on the platforms your customers use, consistently. Facebook and WhatsApp status matter most for Zimbabwean owners.
3. **Cold outreach:** reach people who do not know you yet, with a relevant, personal first message. Volume plus a clear offer.
4. **Paid ads:** pay to put the offer in front of the right people once the offer already converts.

Start with one, do it every day until it works, then add the next.

## Do more, then better, then new

Increase the volume of what works before changing it, improve the quality (hooks, offer, follow-up), then find new channels. Quitting early is the usual failure, not picking the wrong channel.

## Lead getters

Beyond your own effort, leads can come from customers (referrals), employees, affiliates and partners. A simple referral thank-you can outperform an ad.

## Follow-up beats first contact

Speed and persistence in answering enquiries decide who wins the customer. An enquiry answered in minutes converts far better than one answered the next day. Capture contact details so you can follow up, not just hope they come back.

## What to look for on a small business site

- Is there a way to leave contact details, and is anything free offered for them?
- Can someone reach the business in one tap (WhatsApp, call, form), on a phone, on mobile data?
- Is the business easy to find (Google Business profile, search basics, social links)?
- Is there fresh content that proves the business is active and helps people decide?
- Is there any evidence of follow-up after an enquiry?
