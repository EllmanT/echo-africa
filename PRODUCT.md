## Platform

web

## Users
Owners and decision makers of established Zimbabwean and wider African businesses (dealerships, groups, crafts, mining, services) who need a professional website, logo or AI automation and can invest $500 and up. They arrive mostly on phones over mobile data, often from Facebook. They are busy, skeptical of agencies that take deposits and disappear, and read simple English fastest.

## Product Purpose
eka.dev is the one-person agency's funnel: attract owners with free educational content (The Playbook), prove results with real client case studies, qualify them with a short multi-step form, and hand qualified leads to a booked call or WhatsApp. Success is qualified leads at $500+ per project, not traffic.

## Positioning
Zero-risk offer no template shop can copy: Eka builds first and the client pays only when they love it. Real Zimbabwean client results (Faramatsi Motors and Toyota) with measured growth.

## Operating Context
Owner: Tapiwa Muranda, founder, employed elsewhere in parallel, so distribution is Facebook first and no LinkedIn conflict. Deployed on Vercel from GitHub. Currency is USD. WhatsApp is the fastest response channel in the market.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 14.1.4, Tailwind 3, shadcn/ui, framer-motion, MongoDB Atlas, Resend, Cal.com, Anthropic API.
- Light theme only. Purple `#7C3AED` accent (tint `#CBACF9`). Do not switch to dark mode.
- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere in copy. Copy is simple English, outcome first.
- Client figures are published rounded down, with growth multiples, never exact lead counts.
- Scarcity claims only when true (admin-set slots).
- Everything editable content lives in the admin (planned), styling is not editable.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Eka" (eka.dev). Tagline "Only when you love it." Logo at `/public/eka-logo.png` and `/public/eka-logo-2.png`. Footer credit: "Built by Eka.dev".

## Evidence on Hand
Case studies for Faramatsi Motors, Faramatsi Toyota, Mosalex Group, Thee Art Crafts, Kolkart Mining (logo), Excogitate (brand). Client testimonials are WhatsApp screenshots and short quotes. Search Console and lead exports for Faramatsi (May to Aug 2026) live in the sibling `fm-brochure/seo-results/`. No Faramatsi Toyota logo yet. Do not fabricate statistics, reviews or client counts.

## Product Principles
1. Lead with the client's outcome, then the proof, then how fast, then how little effort it took.
2. Give real value first (free Playbook), ask second.
3. Qualify early and politely, never waste a visitor's time or ours.
4. Speed and clarity beat decoration: mobile data first, plain words.
5. Every claim must be true and traceable to a source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable on low-end phones and slow connections; WCAG AA contrast; respects prefers-reduced-motion.
